# Failure and authority boundaries
W/P are guest Job members; M native monitor is outside Job but within guest. C/D/E are logical units in M, not independent watchdogs. R is on a dedicated management station outside guest; physical host/hypervisor, power, network and management-service common modes must be inventoried before independence can be proven.
M hang disables M timers/IPC/storage; M crash/kill differs from hang and does not establish final Job handle close. R uses its own receive clock. R hang cannot be detected/recovered by R itself with a finite guarantee. Another independently enrolled authority or manual operator is a future requirement; no such authority is acquired: guarantee NONE.
Guest network loss/heartbeat loss is suspicion, not proof M/VM/process died. Kernel hang may destroy guest raw records. Hypervisor/R station/network failure removes automatic recovery guarantees. Manual recovery has no automatic time guarantee and is separately logged.
Adversary/faults: stale generation/rollback, reused PID/handle value, replayed nonce, revoked credentials, forged receipt or approval boolean, wrong HEAD/VM/scope, overflowing or malformed IPC, interrupted storage, misleading STOP/API acceptance and later success. Immutable raw/known failures survive later evidence; first failure blocks remaining slots.
Mocks can validate refusal rules, not ACL enforcement, external independence, VM stop, creator-loss rollback, native API behavior or durable storage across power loss.
